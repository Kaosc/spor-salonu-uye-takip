# Member
   - Adding a member
   - Activating a member
   - Deactivating a member 
      - If member has a "ACTIVE" or "PAUSED" subscription, deactivating it will be turn the "ACTIVE" status of the subscription to "CANCELED".

# Subscription
   - Adding a subscription
      - If the member is not active, app will not allow to add a subscription. Staff will need to activate the member first before adding a subscription.
   - Canceling a subscription
   - Pausing a subscription 
   - Unpausing a subscription

# Check-in /  Check-out
   - Member will have a QR Code created by the uid from the firebase auth.
   - Staff can scan the QR Code of the member to
      - Check-in the member
      - Check-out the member
         - Checking-out a member will automatically unassing the locker assigned to the member.
      - View the member's details

# Lockers
   - Members scan Locker QR code to assign a locker to themselves.
   - Order to members to assign a locker, member should be check-in first.
   - When a member checked-out by the staff, the locker will be unassigned automatically.
   - Member unassing the locker by pressing the same button to scan the locker QR code.
   - Staff can unassing a locker from the member's details screen by pressing the "Remove Locker" button.
   - Staff can add a new locker by pressing the "Add Locker" button in the locker screen

# Adding New Staff
  - Only the staff with the "ADMIN" role can add new staff members.
  - The role of "STAFF" cannot add or remove other staff members.

# Login / Register
   - When a member added through the web admin panel, member auth account will be created with the email of the member and a random password. Member will need to reset their password from login screen to access the app by their email adresses.
   - When a member added through the mobile app by staff, a Firebase auth account is created first with a random password. A password reset email is sent to the member. The member doc is created in Firestore using the auth uid as the document ID. Staff is then signed out from the member account and re-authenticated automatically.
   - If a member never created an auth account or never added to member collection by the staff, member will need to register first to create an auth account and then the mobile app will navigate user to the "MemberFormScreen" to fill the member details. After the member details filled, the member will be added to the member collection and linked to the auth account of the member.

# Checkins
   - Checkins saved in the "checkins" collection in Firestore.
   - Checkins Calendar Screen will display the checkins of the members in a calendar view.
   - Pressing a date in the calendar will display the list of checkins of that date.

# User Account Deletion
   - Members can delete their own account from the app. When a member deletes their account, the auth account of the member will be deleted and the member document in the firestore will be cleared from the personal and sensitive information. The member document will still exist in the firestore but with the "isActive" : false.
   - Deleted members' subs will remain in the firestore.
   - If a Staff set member's "isActive" to false, the member will be deactivated and the member will not be able to login to the app. (This is more like deactivating a member and blocking access) The member's subscriptions will remain in the firestore but the active subscription will be canceled automatically.

# Activating / Deactivating Members
   - Staff can activate or deactivate a member from the member details screen.
   - If a member has an active subscription, deactivating the member will pause the active subscription. The member will not be able to login to the app until the member is activated again by the staff.
   - The sub has to be unpaused by the staff after the member is activated again. The member will not be able to unpause the subscription by themselves.
